# Search implementation and next steps

## Implemented

Six distinct pages address the audit, food costs, menu contribution, operating workflow, remote/local delivery, and a practical food-cost guide. Each has unique metadata, a self-referencing lowercase canonical, semantic headings, related links, and relevant Service or Article plus BreadcrumbList structured data. Shared navigation makes every page accessible on mobile and desktop. The sitemap includes all nine indexable pages. No physical address, ratings, client results, profit guarantees or unverified personal biography has been invented. Price currency remains to be confirmed.

The homepage includes the Google verification meta tag from Kirby’s Search Console session. Keep it in place after verification. Use the URL-prefix property https://kmarshall94.github.io/kitchenpilot/ and submit sitemap.xml. Search indexing and ranking are Google’s decisions; technical completion does not promise first place.

2. Add a named founder biography only after Kirby supplies the public name and verified background to publish.
3. Add genuine case studies and testimonials only with client permission and substantiated measurements.
