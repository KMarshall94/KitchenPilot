The problems behind the numbers

Small gaps.
A real cost to your business.

When margins are under pressure, the answer isn’t always more covers. It can be a closer look at what happens between purchasing, prep and the pass.

01 / FOOD COST CONTROL

Your food cost won’t hold steady.

Price changes, inconsistent portioning, poor yields and avoidable waste can pull actual food cost away from what your recipes predict. Purchasing and inventory habits matter just as much as the price on the invoice.

02 / MENU PROFITABILITY

Your best sellers may not be your best earners.

A popular dish can carry an overlooked cost. Recipe costing, ingredient yields, selling prices and sales mix help reveal which core menu items contribute—and which need attention.

03 / OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY

Your team works hard. Service still gets stuck.

Long ticket times, duplicated prep and awkward station handoffs can put pressure on labour and service. We look at workflow, staffing deployment and operational bottlenecks together.

Before we talk

A few useful answers.

Is this relevant if sales are already strong?

Yes. Strong sales can coexist with weak profit. The review focuses on how revenue translates into contribution after food costs and how operating practices may be putting pressure on margins.

What information should I have ready?

Start with what you have: your menu, recipes and portions, supplier invoices, sales mix, inventory records and labour information. We’ll clarify what is needed for the agreed scope. Please don’t send sensitive business records through the initial enquiry form.

What if I don’t know where the problem is?

That’s a useful starting point. Tell us what you’re noticing—rising food costs, uneven margins, excess waste or difficult services. The diagnostic process is designed to help establish where to look and why.

Are profit improvements guaranteed?

No. Findings depend on the quality of the available information, and outcomes depend on implementation and your operating conditions. The aim is a clearer understanding of the issues and practical, informed next steps.
